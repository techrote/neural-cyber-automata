## Independent implementation review

The reviewed implementation preserves the commissioned three-part Jolt star:

- `make_frame` reads each part's own motion, mass and dimensions. Only the root
  receives support, resource and ray sensors. The explicit goal and previous
  actor request are the declared shared context. Evaluator, reward, other-actor
  and cell-patch fields are excluded from the root policy view.
- The model performs one synchronous message round and reads actions only from
  root memory. The no-message model has the same 2,878 parameters and an active
  self-message path. A pad cannot influence the other pad during one update.
- Actor, profile, goal and control/source/mount lifetimes fence private memory
  and queued messages. Cancellation retires the invocation, clears queued
  messages and disarms future requests while the host retains physical motion.
  Unknown, reordered, disabled or detached parts/joints are refused.
- Both learned variants and the authored reference use the common bounded
  six-channel mapping and primary support/cooldown/edge gate. Direct material
  creation, destruction and conversion requests remain zero. Communication
  faults affect controller messages or memory, never the physical joint graph.
- Training consumes eligible training-family demonstrations with explicit
  caller goals. It rejects corrections and privileged teachers, preserves
  unsuccessful trajectories, excludes heldout data, and selects the fixed final
  epoch. Missing or duplicate evaluation rows cannot pass the utility gate.

The original no-go remains appropriate to the recorded result: graph and
no-message each have one normal success in six, and neither trained arrival
required a purposeful hop. A low imitation loss and a passing stress gate at
this low success rate do not establish useful learned hopping or useful
communication. The original budget, declaration, checkpoints and acceptance
thresholds remain unchanged.

## Experiment archive availability

The original record identifies a 379-file, 23,882,471-byte external artifact set,
including all 119 episodes and twelve initial/final checkpoints. The publication
review found no repository releases and no archive retrieval link in the
T-110 evidence or run ledger. The current workspace search also found none of
the original screen summaries, freezes or trajectory archive.

Consequently this review cannot independently repeat the reported raw-artifact
audit, load a historical candidate, or replay its recorded requests. Historical
hashes and the original negative conclusion remain preserved; artifact
availability is **unverified and publication remains pending** under C-005.
The source manifests above close only the source-provenance portion of that gap.

Completion of archive publication requires recovering the original files,
checking them against the recorded hashes, and publishing a stable release-asset
or approved-store link. New training or fabricated replacement outputs would
not recover the original evidence and are not authorized by this review.

The subsequent renewed programme commission declares

implementation and criteria. Its outputs have a separate execution identity and
