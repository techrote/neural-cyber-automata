## Three distinct failure findings

**Recorded controller exception.** The last episode is
`ppo-seed190301-u7-e2` on `teacher-009-right-drop24-r1-h3`. The saved error records
`ValueError` without an exception reason. The
[original training postmortem](jolt-learn-002-training-postmortem-review-20261021.json)
infers the unchanged emergency deadline from frozen source and retained state:
recovery begins at tick 66, eight six-tick overrides reach tick 114, and the
48-tick emergency allowance expires before the next model forward or owner step.
This is a source-and-state inference, not a recovered exception message.
It does not justify relaxing the emergency limit or continuing collection.

The same postmortem identifies two reporting inconsistencies. Failure cleanup
uses suspension as its closure flag, although the saved cancellation fields
satisfy the declared authority-generation and unchanged-state comparisons.
Its default bootstrap fields also say valid/task-terminal despite no attempted
bootstrap. That failed capture was not used in a completed update. These are
future diagnostic/reporting corrections; no original row was rewritten.

**Incomplete original owner journal.** Fresh independent readers find exactly
**6,720 valid chained records**, versus **24,195 claimed by the writer**.
The retained prefix ends with the answered cancel request 2,256, during
`ppo-seed190103-u1-e6`. It contains 2,034 step responses and 12,204
response-reported ticks, 78 episode starts and 77 finishes. No request remains
unanswered within that prefix, but the episode lifetime is unfinished and no
native close, host termination or worker-close record is present.
The missing suffix is 17,475 records relative to the writer's claim; chain tips
also differ. The actual journal SHA-256 is
`0df17e9885eceb41b6e42757557ebad4dfba8adafcb084199245fb8dd5c1ee9c`.

All 757 original inventory entries match their saved bytes, including this
incomplete journal. The discrepancy therefore already existed when the original

inventory cannot prove complete physical evidence. The mechanism that caused

**Missing outer completion.** The terminal file is zero bytes and the executor
and finalization receipts are missing. The frozen executor normally publishes

The full whole-process duration, original executor exit code, timeout status
and independently proven process-tree closure are unknown. No later review
