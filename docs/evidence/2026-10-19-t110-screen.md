### Communication conditions and task limits

| Condition | Authored successes | Graph successes | No-message successes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Normal | 0/6 | 1/6 | 1/6 |
| One-decision message delay | 0/6 | 2/6 | 1/6 |
| 25% seeded edge loss | 0/6 | 1/6 | 1/6 |
| Right-pad communication partition | 0/6 | 3/6 | 1/6 |
| Private-memory reset at tick 60 | 3/6 | 1/6 | 1/6 |

Delay and partition happened to score better than normal graph communication
in this small fixed sample. They were stress conditions, not checkpoint or
architecture choices, and were not selected as a replacement candidate. The
authored and no-message controls have no inter-node edges, so the communication
faults leave their physical task results unchanged; the explicitly declared
private-memory reset can change all three controllers. These runs altered
controller communication or memory, never actual physical parts or joints.

Two limitations make the negative conclusion particularly clear. First, the
normal graph success rate is only 1/6, so a permitted loss of 1/6 is a weak
stress criterion at this level of task reliability. Passing that gate is not
evidence of broadly reliable fault tolerance. Second, the frozen success
criterion requires supported, quiet arrival but does not require a purposeful
launch. Both trained normal successes had `purposeful_launch=false`, despite
one requested primary edge in each episode. The record therefore establishes
arrival outcomes for a directed-hop candidate, without qualifying those two
arrivals as successful hops. No task criterion was rewritten after observing
this limitation.

Owner feel, physical topology mutation, morphology transfer, Windows physical
execution, whole-world replay and general graph-method superiority remain
unmeasured. The native host provides actual motion and applied actuation but
does not provide an independent passive-wrench decomposition; all 19,782 such
tick fields retain their explicit unavailable reason. Zero direct cell-write
requests is a checked action-budget restriction, not a claim of zero native
material evolution or contact motion.
