## Task, reward and stopping rules

The explicit caller goal supplies target, pace 0.8, four-cell tolerance and a four-second intrinsic deadline. Successful arrival requires twelve consecutive actual base ticks within four cells, with speed at most six cells/second, absolute wrapped angle at most 0.32 radians and confirmed support. Success is latched once. A target crossing or an administrative rollout cap is not success.

Reward grants closest-ever target-distance improvement divided by 64, so moving away and returning cannot earn the same approach reward twice. Arrival grants +2 once; an observable task-deadline miss incurs -0.5 once. Time costs 0.05/second; an authored effort proxy costs `0.005 * effort * (abs(move_x)+abs(trim))` per second; excess angle beyond 0.32 radians costs 0.02 per radian/second. Ordinary trim variation costs 0.01 per absolute request change only when the entire interval is supported, cooldown is zero and primary/secondary/auxiliary are inactive. All terms and uncensored component totals are retained separately.

The task evaluator receives actual completed local receipts and remains outside the policy interface. The host's own reward remains explicitly `no_task_reward`; the wrapper records its declared task reward and evaluator outcomes in a separate capture evaluation channel. Policy features never receive that channel.

The host stops at its first terminal tick. Task arrival found inside a six-tick repeat is scored at its actual base tick; the remaining actual motion in that same consumed repeat is retained. The caller issues no later action after task completion. A partial batch cap has a valid final observation and may bootstrap its own value; recurrence never continues across the reset that starts the next optimizer batch. An invalid or missing next observation cannot bootstrap from a stale view. Failed/censored episode artifacts remain in the ledger and evaluation coverage.

## Two learned candidates and controls

Both networks use a 32-wide encoder and GRU cell. Twenty-six common inputs contain normalized target displacement, own velocity, sine/cosine orientation, angular velocity, support/resources/rays with five validity masks, explicit time remaining, pace/tolerance and the previous requested six-channel action. Normalization uses host cells and seconds. Privileged evaluator dictionaries, other actors and hidden target preprocessing are excluded.

Behavioural cloning directly predicts movement X, trim, effort, primary, secondary and auxiliary. Movement Y is zero; its aim uses the same declared directed-hop geometric map as the teacher. Forty fixed training epochs use ordered recurrent windows, squared continuous loss and weighted binary cross entropy. Class weights come only from training demonstrations, clipped to [1, 10]. All eligible teacher samples are retained, including unsuccessful teacher trajectories. Cloning does not silently acquire the teacher's internal state machine.

Residual PPO receives the same common features plus the authored six-channel reference request. It changes only movement X, trim and effort by at most 0.25, 0.20 and 0.15 respectively. The other authored channels remain unchanged. The final combined wire request is bounded. This constrained decision-layer comparison is declared explicitly; a result is not a claim that all action parameterizations are equivalent.

The stochastic residual is a diagonal Gaussian draw followed by a scaled tanh. Capture preserves the original Gaussian draw, bounded residual, baseline request, combined request and the transformed residual density. PPO compares old and new densities of that same sampled residual. It never assigns a clipped wire request, applied impulse, contact response or fallback the latent sample's probability. Likelihood calculation retains the original draw and uses a stable log-Jacobian, including at saturated tanh values. Deterministic evaluation uses the policy mean; the final implementation gives that request no stochastic likelihood and explicitly distinguishes its sampling mode. The original experiment's evaluation-only metadata limitation is retained below.

Every PPO update uses ordered sixteen-step loss windows. The complete preceding episode prefix is reconstructed from zero under current weights without gradient, then detached before the loss window. Padding does not advance the GRU or enter any loss. Actor loss excludes human/model corrections, source takeovers, mixed ownership, refused requests, invalid intervals and deterministic baseline replacements. Reset, actor/profile change, mount/part changes, cancellation and explicit new goal identities fence inference state.

Discount and GAE trace factors are specified per base tick: `gamma=0.997`, `lambda=0.99`, raised to the actual interval length. Value bootstrap, advantage continuation, validity and actor-loss masks are separate. The entropy regularizer is explicitly latent-Gaussian entropy, not a claimed closed-form tanh entropy.

The paired evaluation includes the frozen baseline and idle control once per scenario, and trained/initial BC and residual policies for all three seeds. All learned evaluation requests use the deterministic deployment mapping. BC supplies the direct learned wire-channel comparator; a third direct-action RL configuration is outside this budget. Final epoch/update checkpoints are fixed before opening heldout results.

## Cost and decision gates

The maximum is 13,000 game decisions and 30 minutes wall time across the entire screen, plus a fifteen-minute wall ceiling per training seed. The worst-case planned schedule is 12,864 decisions: 960 demonstration, 6,144 PPO, 1,280 tuning and 4,480 evaluation. Each residual seed receives exactly eight 256-decision batches, 2,048 training decisions total. BC uses forty fixed epochs. CPU PyTorch `2.8.0+cpu`, NumPy `2.3.5` and one Torch thread are declared; no GPU, paid service or automatic budget extension is used.

A useful numeric result needs at least 0.125 absolute paired success-rate improvement. When the baseline already succeeds on at least 0.875 of scenarios, the alternative is at least 0.15 seconds and ten percent improvement in completion time capped at the intrinsic four-second deadline, with no seed losing successes. The selected gain also needs a positive lower percentile-bootstrap bound. Uncertainty resamples the three independently trained policy seeds, using all 27 resamples, rather than treating thousands of transitions as independent training runs. This is an exploratory screen with limited statistical power.

All three seeds must also pass the predeclared mechanical/style/runtime bounds: zero integrity failures or refused requests; no censored evaluation; ordinary trim reversals no more than baseline +0.5/second; mean absolute angle no more than baseline +0.1 radians; peak useful speed between 0.7 and 1.5 times baseline; purposeful-launch fraction no more than 0.125 below baseline; and controller inference p95 at most five milliseconds. Launch evidence requires a consumed primary edge, the pinned pulse's cooldown increase, an actual upward applied impulse and positive energy use followed by airborne motion within twelve ticks. A requested pulse during passive falling is insufficient. Numeric acceptance remains separate from owner feel judgement and real-time deployment qualification.

Outcomes are useful numeric improvement with owner judgement pending, inconclusive at this budget, or no-go at this budget. None requires a favourable PPO result. Idle and untrained results are retained as controls, and administrative/invalid outcomes cannot improve the apparent score through deletion.

Success means the declared twelve-base-tick supported, quiet arrival. Each candidate policy has eight paired scenarios. The authored and idle controls each ran once per scenario and are shared reference observations, not additional independent policy seeds.

BC improved by 37.50 percentage points over its own initial weights; the corresponding policy-seed interval is +27.71 to +47.29 points. Residual PPO improved by 20.83 points over its initial weights, with interval +5.42 to +34.79 points. Initial controls are capable references: every BC policy already uses the declared goal-based aim map, and every residual policy includes the authored reference. The two successes of initial BC seed 1103 are preserved rather than discarded as an inconvenient control.

Average completion time, capped at the four-second deadline, was 3.381 seconds for the authored reference, 3.164 for final BC and 3.114 for final residual PPO. These are descriptive improvements; the actual numerical gate passed through the primary success criterion, since the reference did not satisfy the alternative near-ceiling success condition.

All six final policies passed every declared mechanical/runtime bound. There were no host integrity failures, refused admissions, interventions or censored evaluation episodes. Every seed retained the required useful-speed, orientation and purposeful-launch bounds. The largest ordinary trim reversal rate was 0.392/second for residual seed 1103, below the reference +0.5/second limit. Worst per-episode inference p95 was 3.534 milliseconds among final BC policies and 2.390 milliseconds among final residual policies, below five milliseconds. These measurements include the respective Torch controller's preprocessing, recurrence and wire mapping in this stepped experiment; they do not qualify real-time deployment.

Idle had zero successful arrivals and zero positive returns; its returns ranged from -0.7000 to -0.5719. Closest-ever progress cannot be earned twice by leaving and returning, and the independent quiet-arrival predicate prevents passive target crossings from becoming success. The observed controls and preserved reward components support the task metric's intended interpretation within these fixtures.

Both candidate families therefore have the recorded outcome **numerically useful, owner feel pending** under this fixed exploratory screen. This is evidence of improvement over the selected authored controller at the declared budget. It does not demonstrate general policy superiority, consistently reliable navigation or robust transfer: residual seed 3301 remained at 2/8 successes, and BC seed 1103 succeeded on the four rightward scenarios while failing all four leftward scenarios. Eight fixed scenarios and three policy seeds give limited coverage and statistical power; the intervals resample policy seeds and do not estimate variability over an unrestricted scenario distribution.

### Training and cost

BC's mean training loss decreased from 0.3709 to 0.0789, 0.3653 to 0.0633, and 0.3651 to 0.0706 across the three seeds. The fixed forty epochs took 11.244, 10.651 and 11.037 seconds. Loss reduction is training evidence; the paired actual-host results above establish the measured gameplay outcome.

The twenty-four PPO updates used 5,994 eligible stochastic actor samples out of 6,144 training decisions. Deterministic reference replacements were excluded from actor loss. Total optimizer time was 11.505 seconds, with a median 0.475 seconds per update and range 0.414–0.563 seconds. The 685.600-second screen duration also includes host stepping, reset/transport, capture validation, immutable episode serialization and all comparisons; it must not be reported as deployment inference throughput. Memory, large actor counts, other CPU/platform lanes and GPU performance are unmeasured here.

An actual trained seed-1103 update-eight checkpoint restored the exact policy and all eleven Adam parameter states, whose step counters ranged from 640 to 660. The next sixty-four action RNG draws and scenario choices matched their saved states; the global Torch RNG state also matched. A new controller had zero hidden state. This is a verified fresh-episode checkpoint load and state restoration. No additional resumed game rollout or exact mid-episode host snapshot is claimed.

### Contamination and preserved metadata limitation

The [postprocessing auditor](../../evals/first_skill/audit.py) verified every episode file and record hash, all thirty-four original source-file hashes, the single physical profile, matched per-scenario configuration, unchanged checkpoints, all declared partitions and the complete teacher dataset. It found zero interventions, invalid intervals or refused admissions. The persistent host's monotonic receipts place all training capture before diagnostic capture; all optimizer update ledgers precede the immutable checkpoint freeze. Every admitted training actor-sample count matches its update ledger. **No tuning or evaluation episode entered a training sequence or optimizer update.**

The original learner had one evaluation-only metadata defect: deterministic residual mean requests retained the Normal density evaluated at that mean. This caused 416 diagnostic-tuning and 1,555 holdout decisions to advertise generic on-policy eligibility despite having no stochastic draw. Those preserved records must not be reused as stochastic PPO samples. The frozen screen constructed rollout sequences only inside `training=True`, which is enabled solely for training-family PPO collection, so the label defect did not contaminate its updates or alter its deterministic wire requests, weights, rewards or task outcomes.

The final implementation removes the likelihood from deterministic mean inference, records an explicit deterministic distribution and sampling mode, and requires `stochastic_draw` before residual actor attribution. Missing/unknown modes and deterministic means are refused. The original artifacts and source identity remain unchanged. A separate publication fix lets `freeze_manifest` use accepted automatic source discovery instead of imposing a source-tree path on installed packages. These post-run metadata/packaging corrections are distinguished from the source that trained and evaluated the policies.

### Downstream gate decisions

The morphology prerequisite was independently recorded at 01:32:08 UTC, before holdout opening, with declaration hash `6d4b567e99308d57e0dfb076e2137ea693112ab8bf4ea30af144111bb9516654`. It requires mean success at least 0.75 and every seed at least 0.50, in addition to positive improvement over initial controls and the original character bounds. BC's mean/minimum are 0.4583/0.375; residual PPO's are 0.5000/0.250. **Neither family passes that prerequisite.** No morphology training was started, and the gate is not relaxed after observing these outcomes.

Runtime export requires a trained and evaluated candidate rather than morphology-level reliability. BC seed 1103 was selected for that separate qualification lane before holdout results were inspected. Its weights and evaluation evidence can support the next bounded CPU export experiment, while its measured leftward failures, owner-judgement status and deployment limits remain explicit.
