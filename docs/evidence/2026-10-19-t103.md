# Authored controls and response fixtures

## Implemented behavior

[AuthoredController](../../src/nca/controllers/baseline.py) supplies three named
skills: Jolt (`jev`) `directed_hop`, Vector (`astra`) `burst_traverse`, and the
actor-specific `recover_level`. Only these two actor mappings are declared.

Jolt aims a committed pulse, requires fresh stable support before issuing its
rising edge, withholds repeated pulses during flight/cooldown, prepares a rebound
from observed descent/contact proximity, and uses the existing self-right button
and bounded trim when tipped. This follows the host's actual pulse semantics;
Jolt is not a release-to-launch charging rig.

Vector uses its existing vector drive, independently aimed pulse and counterthrust
button. It brakes before reversal and near its target. Its auxiliary button
switches coast mode, so this controller does not misinterpret it as self-right.
The host's existing gyro, joints, energy cost and material owner remain common
to every source. The controller never adds a private force or pose setter.

[Request shaping](../../src/nca/controllers/shaping.py) provides hysteretic
deadbands, asymmetric rates expressed per simulated second, bounded deterministic
play memory, immediate cancellation and an optional mapped-joint soft-stop target
function. Request play is not a second mechanical simulation. Shared helpers can
shape any source. The three [resolved tuning variants](../../configs/baselines/)
change feedback requests; they do not secretly change host assistance.

Cancellation clears the pending command and pulse and bypasses stale smoothing,
while physical motion remains in the host. Reusing the canceled goal cannot
restart it. A fresh invocation is required; episode reset reconstructs controller
memory. The caller also invokes host cancellation, since a neutral wire packet
alone does not revoke already queued owner input. Terminal suspension is distinct
from invalid final sensor data.

## Matched physical pilots

Eighteen runs used seed **1703**, two actors, three tuning variants, and the same
step/pulse/reversal command definitions. Each policy decision spans six 60 Hz
base ticks; the command lease remains twelve observation-anchored ticks. The
combined budget was **1,080 decisions / 6,480 base ticks**, taking **37.064 seconds**
of measured pilot wall time, including process setup, resets and receipt capture.
This is a bounded fixture throughput observation, not a training benchmark.

The fixed-target step asks for +48 cells horizontally from the actor's initial
position. Arrival requires a distance at most four cells and speed at most six
cells/s for a 0.2-second controller dwell. Arrival is a local skill estimate and
is not an evaluator-provided policy feature. Response onset below means the first
10 Hz sample with at least 2 cells/s toward the target; its resolution is 0.1 s.

| Actor | Tuning | Response onset | First local arrival | Peak speed (cells/s) | Net horizontal progress at 6 s | Corrective trim reversals |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jolt | firm | 0.3 s | 1.4 s | 68.48 | 46.54 cells | 1 |
| Jolt | balanced | 0.3 s | 1.4 s | 59.81 | 45.82 cells | 0 |
| Jolt | compliant | 0.2 s | 1.3 s | 67.56 | 45.78 cells | 1 |
| Vector | firm | 0.1 s | 1.9 s | 47.77 | 44.32 cells | 0 |
| Vector | balanced | 0.1 s | 1.6 s | 43.17 | 43.70 cells | 0 |
| Vector | compliant | 0.1 s | 1.6 s | 51.42 | 44.23 cells | 0 |

All six step traces had zero invalid motion samples and no observed overshoot
past the target along the command axis. This does not imply zero 2D target error.
The requested-rate-limited fraction is kept distinct from host actuator
saturation. Corrective trim reversals exclude deliberate launch, flight,
recoil and recovery phases; they are not a blanket total-motion smoothness score.

The 0.5-second command pulse followed by owner cancellation did not reach the
target in any variant, as expected for interrupted intent. It is a cancellation
fixture, not a skill-success trial. Existing momentum continues under the host.

The reversal starts the second target at tick 180. Jolt firm reached it at tick
354, balanced at 378; **compliant did not reach it by tick 480**. Vector firm and
balanced reached it at tick 312, compliant at 306. The compliant Jolt result is
retained as a failure at this scenario and budget; its faster initial response
does not make it an unconditional preferred tuning.

Vector reaches the target, finishes its invocation and then coasts. All three
Vector step runs subsequently drift outside the arrival region; whole-run
settling is therefore **unobserved**, not zero. `burst_traverse` is an arrival
skill, not sustained target holding or a hover controller. Balanced Jolt is a
useful initial learning reference, while alternative tuning remains available.
These single-seed fixture observations do not establish a statistical ranking.

## Physical fixture and owner limits

The [fixture definitions](../../configs/fixtures/) also specify neutral
disturbance, forced support loss/return, soft end regions, recoil/payload changes
and recovery. Their negative and lifecycle checks run on supplied observations
or pure request shaping. The accepted arena has no public arbitrary disturbance,
support removal, payload replacement or joint-profile override interface.
`CybersandFixtureHost` refuses those operations instead of passing them as no-ops.
Forced-perturbation physical qualification consequently remains unmeasured.

Actual Jolt launch/contact transitions occur in the ordinary pilots. They do not
substitute for intentionally removing support mid-stance. Native usable recoil
can be requested through the tool channel on a suitable mounted actor; an
arbitrary requested recoil vector is a different fixture and is not silently
mapped to that operation. A host-side fixture task and acceptance process can
expose additional owner-controlled operations without moving the plant here.

The remaining controlled physical matrix, game-rendered feel review and owner
judgement remain separate from this bounded
software and physical pilot evidence. No learned policy or all-rig qualification
is claimed by this record.
