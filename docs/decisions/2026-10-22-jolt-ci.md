# D-021 — Explicitly gated long-running Jolt CI entry

[ACCEPTED] 2026-10-22 by owner, after reporting PR #76 CI green but
no long experiment runs: implement the missing real LEARN-JOLT-003 CI
execution path. This lifts D-020's instruction to stop development after
conformance CI; it does **not** waive pending resource admission or authorize
an unreviewed physical invocation.

The [manual workflow](../../.github/workflows/learn-jolt-003-long-run.yml)
has separate read-only preflight and prospective execute modes. Only execute
may freeze a full source/runtime/resource-bound declaration with the two
independent passing final reviews, publish and read back its exact original
bytes, and then claim the sole allocation durably on issue #62 **before**
calling the real observer/worker. A failed, cancelled or incomplete invocation
cannot be silently retried from another ephemeral runner or by pressing
GitHub's rerun button. Execution is restricted to the original CPU workload
and unchanged 39 counters, seeds, case order, integrity and qualification
gates; no GPU or new experiment budget is commissioned.

The CI workflow always attempts to publish original/partial evidence,
observed exit, execution receipts and SHA-256 inventory. Upload is not
permanent external-archive qualification and cannot be guaranteed after
hard infrastructure termination. GitHub-hosted execution still has a
platform-enforced six-hour job maximum, regardless of configuration v2.
Memory feasibility and fresh prospective reviews remain unresolved.
Green conformance tests do not imply admission, trained checkpoints or a
qualified learned actor.
