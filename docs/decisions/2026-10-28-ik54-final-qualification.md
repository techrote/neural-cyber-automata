# D-035 — Candidate-bound final qualification

[ACCEPTED SCOPE] On 28 October 2026 the owner instructed: “Prepare the separate
final-suite declaration, obtain the required independent admissions, and run
the final qualification if all checks pass.” This is the separate final-access
authority anticipated by D-034. It does not authorize new training, tuning-based
reselection, retries, relaxed thresholds, or altering prior evidence.

Use the three already frozen tuning-selected checkpoints and their actual
initial checkpoints. A separate single-use evaluation allocation must bind the
current source, original sealed polar suite provenance, exact candidate bytes,
all six declared comparators, CPU timing, finite resource ceilings and two fresh
independent non-author admissions. The original 20 GiB aggregate TITAN board
watchdog and V100 prohibition remain. A durable candidate-bound opening must
precede archive decoding; a failure retains its claim and does not permit a
retry. No final payload is accessed while preparing or reviewing the packet.

The five official comparators retain their unchanged gates. The archived v1
checkpoint is a sixth, diagnostic-only comparator; it cannot influence candidate
selection. Outcomes, including no-go or failure, must be reported without
reclassification. Physical tracking and runtime export remain separately gated.

This decision authorizes preparation and conditional execution; it is not an
admission, suite opening, final result or acceptance claim.
