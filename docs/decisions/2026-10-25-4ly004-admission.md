# D-032 — Finish the 4LY-004 experiment-admission package

[ACCEPTED] 2026-10-25 by owner: finish #74's experiment-admission package
after bridge PR #85 merged.

Freeze the exact public development/tuning manifests, Ubuntu 24.04 Linux x86_64
CPU learner contract, clean-source binding and finite one-attempt resource
envelope without starting capture or training. The resource policy is 18,000 s
whole-attempt wall time, 4 GiB process-tree RSS, 8 GiB retained attempt storage
and 4 GiB publication bytes, enforced fail-stop by a continuously running
resource guard tied to the declaration. These are allocation ceilings, not
claims of measured learner consumption, and authorize no extension or retry.

Do not generate, open or store learner-final payload bytes in this repository.
Prospective execution still requires an external custodian/private reviewer to
commit an unopened, disjoint and feasible 60-case learner-final suite, then a
distinct independently authored execution review bound to the exact
source/splits/runtime/resource/custody candidate. Routine CI may prepare the
review candidate but cannot self-admit the experiment. Tuning-only checkpoint
selection and continuously advancing export qualification remain
post-admission gates. Teacher-final reuse, CUDA, sweeps and automatic retries
remain forbidden.
