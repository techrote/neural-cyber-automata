# Same-candidate IK evaluation recovery

[ACCEPTED D-026] The owner explicitly commissions fixing the archive path,
preparing a reviewed recovery amendment preserving markers, charges and
allocations, obtaining two fresh admissions and finishing evaluation of the
same frozen candidates on TITAN RTX only. No retraining is authorized.

The prior final-opening receipt and its development-only flag remain unchanged.
All failed supervisor results, overrun markers, original declarations, admissions,
training checkpoints and charges remain immutable historical evidence. This is
recovery of that same experiment and frozen candidate set, not a fresh final
suite, replacement allocation or new candidate selection.

The recovery amendment must authenticate the original archive layout, prior
failed outcome, opening and binding, every retained run file and identical
frozen candidates. The failed process and publication cost is conservatively
accounted inside each original wall ceiling by an append-only recovery record.
No time, call, checkpoint or attempt ceiling increases. Only exact hash-bound
historical failure records can be reconciled; other uncertainty still refuses.
Future failed recovery work does not inherit this exception.

Existing ordinary training readers and execution entry points retain their
refusals. Recovery-only authenticated access may reopen original train/tuning
bytes to reconstruct the unchanged model normalization and selected checkpoints,
solely for this already-opened experiment and its exact frozen identities.
It must not remove or hide the marker or expose training privileges. A separate
recovery binding records actual new execution source without rewriting the old
opening or binding.

Correct member lookup uses the authenticated archive's declared top directory.
Original shard bytes, all comparators, denominators, numerical thresholds,
one-thread CPU timing protocol and original allocations remain unchanged.
Fresh exact-source CPU checks, CI, an immutable recovery declaration and two
genuine independent admissions precede CUDA or final payload extraction.
Only the fingerprint-bound TITAN RTX is visible to CUDA; the V100 is prohibited.

Completion requires independent verification of costs, ledger/checkpoint
integrity, unchanged model/optimizer state, final populations, timing and
aggregate verdicts. A no-go or inconclusive quality result is still reported
as such. Recovery authorization does not permit retuning or final-driven learning.
